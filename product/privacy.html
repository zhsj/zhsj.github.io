Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

This policy applies to all software, applications, websites and services published by Shengjing Zhu, including anything released in the future. It does not cover services run by third parties.

1. Your data

We collect no personal data and sell or share none. There are no accounts, analytics or tracking.

Settings and credentials stay on your device. Content stays there or moves only between you and the services you connect using your own credentials. We never receive a copy.

2. Third-party services

Third parties host our pages and downloads and operate the services you connect. They follow their own terms and privacy policies and may log technical information; we do not receive it.

3. Children

Our software and websites are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

4. Your controls

Clear local data in the application’s settings or by uninstalling it, revoke access in the provider’s settings, and delete what you posted publicly.

5. Changes

We will update this policy before we start collecting personal data. The effective date changes with each update.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue in the relevant project’s repository on GitHub.
